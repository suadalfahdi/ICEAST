About the Conference

Aim and Scope

The International Conference on Engineering Advancements, Science and Technology (ICEAST) was established by the Military Technological College (MTC), Sultanate of Oman, as a multidisciplinary platform dedicated to promoting excellence in research, innovation, and technological development. Since its inception, ICEAST has provided a forum for academics, researchers, industry professionals, and policymakers to exchange knowledge, showcase emerging technologies, and discuss solutions to contemporary engineering and scientific challenges.

ICSETS 2027

2nd International Conference on Engineering Systems, Technology & Sustainable Solutions

Under the ICEAST 2027 umbrella
31 October – 4 November 2027 · Muscat, Oman

The 2nd International Conference on Systems Engineering, Technology & Sustainable Solutions serves as a premier platform for researchers, engineers, academics, and industry professionals to exchange knowledge and present innovations in intelligent systems, advanced technologies, and sustainable engineering solutions. The conference addresses the growing need for integrated engineering approaches that support digital transformation, resilience, sustainability, and technological advancement.

ICSETS 2027 focuses on emerging developments in artificial intelligence, autonomous systems, digital engineering, cybersecurity, advanced manufacturing, energy technologies, and biomedical innovations. By fostering interdisciplinary collaboration and innovation, the conference aims to contribute to the development of future-ready engineering solutions that support industrial growth, societal wellbeing, and the objectives of sustainable development.

ICASET 2027

3rd International Conference on Aeronautical Sciences, Engineering & Technology

Under the ICEAST 2027 umbrella
31 October – 4 November 2027 · Muscat, Oman

2027 ThemeShaping the Future of Aviation and Space: Innovation, Sustainability and Intelligent Technologies

The 3rd International Conference on Aeronautical Sciences, Engineering & Technology provides an international forum for researchers, practitioners, industry leaders, and policymakers to explore advances in aeronautics, aerospace systems, and aviation technologies. The conference promotes innovation across civil, commercial, and defense aviation sectors while addressing the challenges and opportunities shaping the future of aerospace engineering.

ICASET 2027 covers a broad spectrum of topics including aerodynamics, propulsion systems, intelligent avionics, autonomous aerospace technologies, sustainable aviation, future air mobility, unmanned aerial systems, aviation safety, and space technologies. The conference seeks to showcase cutting-edge research and facilitate collaboration among academia, industry, and government organizations to advance aerospace excellence and innovation.

ICMET 2027

3rd International Conference on Marine Engineering & Technology

Under the ICEAST 2027 umbrella
31 October – 4 November 2027 · Muscat, Oman

2027 ThemeAdvancing Smart and Sustainable Marine Technologies for Oman’s Blue Economy and Maritime Future

The 3rd International Conference on Marine Engineering & Technology (ICMET) brings together researchers, maritime professionals, naval architects, marine engineers, industry experts, and policymakers to discuss emerging technologies and sustainable solutions for the maritime sector. As global attention increasingly turns toward the blue economy, smart shipping, and marine sustainability, ICMET provides a vital platform for knowledge exchange and collaboration.

The conference focuses on sustainable marine technologies, naval architecture, digital transformation in maritime operations, marine safety, environmental protection, and smart maritime infrastructure. Through its multidisciplinary approach, ICMET 2027 aims to support the development of innovative, efficient, and environmentally responsible maritime systems that contribute to economic growth and sustainable ocean resources management.

04 / 05
ICSBC 2027

4th International Conference on Sustainable Buildings & Construction

Under the ICEAST 2027 umbrella
31 October – 4 November 2027 · Muscat, Oman

The 4th International Conference on Sustainable Buildings & Construction serves as a leading forum for researchers, engineers, architects, planners, policymakers, and industry professionals to discuss advancements in sustainable construction and the built environment. As cities face increasing challenges related to urbanization, climate change, and resource management, ICSBC promotes innovative solutions that enhance sustainability, resilience, and infrastructure performance.

The conference focuses on digital transformation in construction, intelligent infrastructure systems, sustainable engineering practices, resource optimization, smart cities, advanced geomatics, structural assessment, conservation, and resilient urban development. ICSBC 2027 aims to drive collaboration between academia and industry while supporting the development of sustainable, efficient, and future-ready buildings and infrastructure systems.

05 / 05
ICSELL 2027

2nd International Conference on Science & English Language Learning

Under the ICEAST 2027 umbrella
31 October – 4 November 2027 · Muscat, Oman

2027 ThemeAI-Driven Innovations in Science and Language Learning: Bridging Pedagogy and Technology

The 2nd International Conference on Science & English Language Learning (ICSELL) provides a unique interdisciplinary platform that bridges scientific research, educational innovation, and language learning. The conference brings together educators, researchers, scientists, technology specialists, and practitioners to explore innovative approaches to teaching, learning, and scientific advancement in a rapidly evolving digital world.

ICSELL 2027 addresses contemporary challenges and opportunities in English language learning, digital pedagogy, educational innovation, artificial intelligence in education, physical sciences, and mathematical modelling. By integrating education, science, and technology, the conference seeks to foster innovative teaching practices, strengthen STEM learning, and support the development of future-ready learners equipped with the skills required for the knowledge economy.
