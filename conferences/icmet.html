About the Conference

3rd International Conference on Marine Engineering & Technology

The 3rd International Conference on Marine Engineering & Technology (ICMET) brings together researchers, maritime professionals, naval architects, marine engineers, industry experts, and policymakers to discuss emerging technologies and sustainable solutions for the maritime sector. As global attention increasingly turns toward the blue economy, smart shipping, and marine sustainability, ICMET provides a vital platform for knowledge exchange and collaboration.

The conference focuses on sustainable marine technologies, naval architecture, digital transformation in maritime operations, marine safety, environmental protection, and smart maritime infrastructure. Through its multidisciplinary approach, ICMET 2027 aims to support the development of innovative, efficient, and environmentally responsible maritime systems that contribute to economic growth and sustainable ocean resources management.
