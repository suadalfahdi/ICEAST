About the Conference

2nd International Conference on Science & English Language Learning

The 2nd International Conference on Science & English Language Learning (ICSELL) provides a unique interdisciplinary platform that bridges scientific research, educational innovation, and language learning. The conference brings together educators, researchers, scientists, technology specialists, and practitioners to explore innovative approaches to teaching, learning, and scientific advancement in a rapidly evolving digital world.

ICSELL 2027 addresses contemporary challenges and opportunities in English language learning, digital pedagogy, educational innovation, artificial intelligence in education, physical sciences, and mathematical modelling. By integrating education, science, and technology, the conference seeks to foster innovative teaching practices, strengthen STEM learning, and support the development of future-ready learners equipped with the skills required for the knowledge economy.
