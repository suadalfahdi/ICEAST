About the Conference

4th International Conference on Sustainable Buildings & Construction

The 4th International Conference on Sustainable Buildings & Construction serves as a leading forum for researchers, engineers, architects, planners, policymakers, and industry professionals to discuss advancements in sustainable construction and the built environment. As cities face increasing challenges related to urbanization, climate change, and resource management, ICSBC promotes innovative solutions that enhance sustainability, resilience, and infrastructure performance.

The conference focuses on digital transformation in construction, intelligent infrastructure systems, sustainable engineering practices, resource optimization, smart cities, advanced geomatics, structural assessment, conservation, and resilient urban development. ICSBC 2027 aims to drive collaboration between academia and industry while supporting the development of sustainable, efficient, and future-ready buildings and infrastructure systems.
