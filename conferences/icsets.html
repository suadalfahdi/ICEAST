About the Conference

2nd International Conference on Engineering Systems, Technology & Sustainable Solutions

The 2nd International Conference on Systems Engineering, Technology & Sustainable Solutions serves as a premier platform for researchers, engineers, academics, and industry professionals to exchange knowledge and present innovations in intelligent systems, advanced technologies, and sustainable engineering solutions. The conference addresses the growing need for integrated engineering approaches that support digital transformation, resilience, sustainability, and technological advancement.

ICSETS 2027 focuses on emerging developments in artificial intelligence, autonomous systems, digital engineering, cybersecurity, advanced manufacturing, energy technologies, and biomedical innovations. By fostering interdisciplinary collaboration and innovation, the conference aims to contribute to the development of future-ready engineering solutions that support industrial growth, societal wellbeing, and the objectives of sustainable development.
