Call for Reviewers

ICEAST 2027 invites distinguished researchers, academicians, and industry professionals to contribute as reviewers. Your expertise will ensure the quality, integrity, and global impact of the conference proceedings.

Apply to Become a Reviewer Reviewer Guidelines

Who Can Apply

Reviewer Eligibility Criteria

Academic Qualifications

  • Hold a PhD degree in a relevant discipline; or
  • Be currently pursuing a PhD with demonstrated research experience; or
  • Hold a Master's degree with significant professional and research experience with more than 12 years of professional experience.

Research Experience

  • Authored or co-authored peer-reviewed journal or conference publications.
  • Demonstrated expertise in one or more conference topics.
  • Experience in reviewing manuscripts for journals or conferences is desirable.

Professional Experience

  • Academic staff members at recognized higher education institutions.
  • Researchers from research centers or laboratories.
  • Experienced professionals from industry, government, defense, or professional organizations with relevant technical expertise.

Your Role

Reviewer Responsibilities

Reviewers are expected to:

  • Evaluate assigned manuscripts objectively and fairly.
  • Assess submissions for originality, technical quality, significance, and relevance.
  • Provide constructive comments to help authors improve their work.
  • Submit reviews within the stipulated deadlines.
  • Maintain the confidentiality of all submitted manuscripts.
  • Declare any conflict of interest before accepting a review assignment.
  • Adhere to the ethical standards of the conference and publishing partners.

Recognition

Reviewer Benefits

Selected reviewers will receive:

  • Official Certificate of Appreciation from ICEAST 2027.
  • Recognition on the conference website (subject to consent).
  • Opportunity to contribute to an international academic event.
  • Professional networking opportunities with researchers and experts worldwide.
  • Consideration for future Technical Program Committees and Editorial Roles.
  • Priority consideration for serving as Track Chairs and Session Chairs in future editions.

Expertise

Review Areas

Applicants are encouraged to indicate their expertise areas based on the conference tracks, including:

  • Artificial Intelligence and Intelligent Systems
  • Systems Engineering
  • Electronics and Communications
  • Sustainable Energy
  • Cybersecurity
  • Advanced Manufacturing
  • Aeronautical Engineering
  • Unmanned Aerial Systems
  • Marine Engineering
  • Naval Architecture
  • Digital Education
  • English Language Learning
  • Physics
  • Mathematics
  • Sustainable Construction
  • Geomatics
  • Smart Cities
  • Infrastructure Engineering
  • Other specialized areas

Standards

Reviewer Guidelines

!

Strict Policy: The use of AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini) for generating or modifying review content is strictly prohibited.

Core Ethical Principles

  • Maintain strict confidentiality of submitted manuscripts
  • Ensure impartial and unbiased evaluation
  • Provide scientifically rigorous and evidence-based feedback
  • Adhere to professional and ethical standards

Conflict of Interest

Reviewers must decline assignments if there is any conflict of interest, including:

  • Same institution or recent collaboration
  • Personal or professional relationships
  • Financial or competitive conflicts

Review Process

  • Double-blind peer review system
  • Evaluation based solely on technical merit

Evaluation Criteria

  • Originality and novelty
  • Technical correctness and depth
  • Experimental validation and results
  • Clarity of presentation
  • Relevance to conference themes

Final Recommendation Categories

  • Accept
  • Borderline
  • Reject

Reviewer Application

The Organising Committee invites qualified academics, researchers, practitioners, and subject specialists to apply as paper reviewers. Reviewer selection will be based on qualifications, research and reviewing experience, areas of expertise, publication record, and alignment with the conference tracks.
